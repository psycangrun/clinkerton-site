Clinkerton Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Clinkerton does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your runs, charms, upgrades and settings in a file on your device. That file never leaves your device; we can't see it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • We don't collect usage data, device identifiers or location.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising or analytics services.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share any data, because we don't have any.

Copying your run history

The game's Run History has a Copy button. It copies a text summary of your runs to your device's clipboard so you can paste it where you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste and send it yourself.

Children

Clinkerton contains simulated gambling and is rated for adults. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

No real money

Clinkerton is a game. There is no real-money wagering, no cash prizes and nothing to buy. Chips have no value outside the game.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a future version adds optional ads, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
